# Development roadmap

1. Foundation: solution, Angular shell, PostgreSQL migration, health and catalog read. Verified locally.
2. Identity: customer registration and login, permission-based authorization, recovery and verification.
3. Catalog and stock: administration, search, images and inventory.
4. Commerce: cart, addresses, transactional checkout, orders, cancellation and payment records.
5. Services: staff schedules, booking availability, collision-proof booking, reschedule and cancellation.
6. Integrations and content: payment and shipping adapters, media, blog, reviews and notifications.
7. Production readiness: E2E, security and concurrency tests, backups, monitoring, HTTPS and deployment.

Each phase must pass build and relevant tests before being marked complete. External integrations remain pending until accounts and credentials are supplied.
